LedBox

Neon arcade survival for Android

The network is infected. You are its last antivirus. Dodge corrupted packets, dash through danger and survive as long as you can.

A rede foi infectada. Você é o último antivírus: desvie, dê dash e sobreviva o máximo que puder.

  • Free to play
  • Online leaderboard
  • Play with a friend

Dodge

Red warnings show where corrupted packets will pass. Read them, move, survive. Your score is the time you stay alive.

Dash

Move with your left thumb, tap the right side to dash. For a split second, nothing can hurt you.

Collect

Grab bits during the run and unlock skins, colors and trails. Seasonal events bring limited items.

Compete

Climb the online leaderboard or create a room and challenge a friend with the same meteors.

The infection keeps mutating. Survive long enough and a boss shows up, each one with its own attacks and its own weakness.
