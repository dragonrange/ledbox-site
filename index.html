Free to play · Online leaderboard · Play with a friend

How long can you last?

Dodge

Red warnings show where corrupted packets will pass. Read them, move, survive. Your score is the time you stay alive.

Dash

Move with your left thumb, tap the right side to dash. For a split second, nothing can hurt you.

Collect & customize

Grab bits during the run and unlock skins, colors and trails. Seasonal events bring limited items.

Compete

Climb the online leaderboard or create a room and challenge a friend with the same meteors.

Boss

OHMEGA

The root virus

Survive 90 seconds and the core of the infection shows up. Its cannons fill the screen with bullets, but it has one weakness: dash into the purple patches and send them back at it.
